Features

Everything an email client should do.
And nothing it shouldn't.

Built for people who read a lot of mail and want to get through it fast — from the keyboard, with no waiting and no noise.

Every account, one inbox

Gmail, Outlook, iCloud, Yahoo or any IMAP server, side by side. An "All inboxes" view brings it together; each account keeps its own colour.

Mail in real time

IMAP IDLE: a message arrives the second it is sent, with no polling. Lives in the system tray, with a badge and a sound — when you want them.

Quiet hours and snooze

Decide when you don't want to be interrupted, with an exception for the senders who matter. Snooze a message until later, tomorrow or next week.

Triage from the keyboard

Archive, delete, star, move, next unread — all one key away. Search with operators: from:, subject:, has:attachment, before:.

Sign in with Google or Microsoft

Sign in through your browser, the way it should be — or use an app password if you prefer. Sidsmail finds the servers on its own.

Small things that count

Reply templates, per-account signatures, word count, a Trash that empties itself, light and dark themes, an accent colour of your choosing.

Privacy

Your mail stays on your computer.

Sidsmail talks directly to your account's server. There is no server of ours in between, no Sidsmail account, no telemetry. What you keep, you keep.

  • Remote images blocked by default — newsletter tracking pixels never load unless you say so.
  • Passwords encrypted with Windows' own data protection, tied to your user profile.
  • Optional PIN lock at startup and when returning from the tray.
  • Export or erase everything in one click — the data is yours, including the right to take it with you.

FAQ

Before you ask.

Does it work with Gmail?

Yes. Either sign in with Google (one click in your browser) or use an app password — Gmail refuses your normal password in every email client, not just Sidsmail.

And Outlook and Hotmail?

Yes, by signing in with Microsoft. Microsoft no longer accepts app passwords on personal accounts, so signing in through the browser is the only way — Sidsmail handles the rest.

Where is my data kept?

In %APPDATA%\Sidsmail, on your computer: accounts, settings and a local copy of your messages for reading offline. Uninstalling never touches that folder; "Erase local data", in Settings, removes it entirely.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Sidsmail is free and has no "Pro" edition. If there is ever a way to support the project, it will appear here — never inside the app, interrupting you.

HTML messages only show as plain text?

Sidsmail uses Windows' WebView2 to render HTML. It ships with Windows 11 and most up-to-date Windows 10 installs; if it is missing, install it here — meanwhile the app shows everything as plain text, nothing is lost.
